# Management action follow-up

For each investigated project exception, capture the project identifier, reporting period, observed KPI, source evidence, proposed action, accountable owner and review date. Distinguish an observed variance from a hypothesized cause. For example, a positive cost variance alone does not establish that staffing caused the overrun; inspect finance and timesheet evidence at the appropriate grain first.

At follow-up, record whether the action is open, blocked or complete and what evidence supports the status. Recheck the KPI using a comparable reporting period. Do not present the hypothetical intervention as a measured business benefit from this synthetic portfolio.
